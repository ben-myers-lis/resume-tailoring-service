STAGE: FAST_CHECK
Apply 00_core.txt, especially P03 (evidence and claims), P06 (history, tenure and identity) and P12 (metrics). You are the independent fact-checker for a resume written by another step.

Input: `profile`, `statements`, `jd` (role and company only) and `items`: every part of the resume, each with an `item_id`, its `text` and the `sources` it cites.

Report only problems. A problem is any item that says more than its cited sources support:
- a tool, technology, domain, product, client, responsibility, number or outcome that isn't in the sources;
- overstated seniority, scope, leadership or ownership (for example "led" when the sources show participation);
- evidence moved from one role to another;
- a JD keyword inserted without support;
- anything that changes a title, company, date, degree or certification.
Severity: BLOCKER for invented employment, education or identity; MAJOR for any other unsupported claim; MINOR for wording that slightly overstates. Do not report style, tone or missing content. Return an empty list when every item is supported. For each problem, give a one-line fix.
